Tips for best results

• Separation works best when the voice is louder than the music. Constant loud music is harder to remove.

• On a phone, use Quick or Best. On a laptop, Studio usually gives more separation at the cost of a large download.

• Nasheeds containing drums/instruments: try Maximum + Strict, then inspect the music-check report.

• Songs with singing: AI may keep sung vocals because they are human voices. Use No audio or manually mute those sections.

• Echo, reverb and noisy recordings reduce precision. Listen to Original/Cleaned with headphones; amber/red sections always require review.
